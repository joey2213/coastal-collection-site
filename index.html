805–835 Trouville Avenue, Grover Beach

The Coastal Collection is four new residences on a quiet Grover Beach street — blocks from the ocean, moments from 4th Street. Each home is approximately 1,743 square feet: three bedrooms, two baths, a private two-car garage, and living that opens to a front deck and a private roof above.

Final touches are underway. The street is small on purpose. The rest of the day belongs to the coast.

The Coastal Collection of Grover Beach

Our Story

A small collection
on Trouville.

The Coastal Collection is four new residences at 805–835 Trouville Avenue in Grover Beach. Not a hillside gated neighborhood. A street — close to the water, close to 4th Street, sized so you can know every house from the sidewalk.

Two floor plans share the same 1,743 square feet, three bedrooms, two baths, and a two-car garage. Vista takes the ends and the extra windows. Garden keeps the primary suite downstairs. Both open to a front deck and a private roof.

Final touches are underway. The next chapter is whoever walks the street and stays.

The rest of the day belongs to the coast.

S & S Homes of the Central Coast

Built by S & S Homes of the Central Coast, Arroyo Grande. Four residences on Trouville Avenue — a small collection, finished as the coast requires.

How the homes are kept

Light. Privacy. The roof.

Light

Open living, extra glass on the end homes, rooms that run to the front deck. The coast is the north star — the interiors are built to take it in.

Privacy

Four attached homes, not one long room. Construction between the residences is planned for sound separation and a quieter neighbor.

The roof

A private rooftop deck on every home, engineered for a hot tub, held for evenings that do not need an errand.
